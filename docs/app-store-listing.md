# App Store listing: localized text
Draft copy for each language the app ships in. **Not uploaded anywhere**: paste it into App Store Connect (or ask for it to be applied) when the listing is ready. Limits: name 30, subtitle 30, promotional text 170, keywords 100, description 4000 characters.
The app name stays `MURMUR` in every language.

## English (U.S.) (`en`)

**Subtitle** (27/30): Voice notes, on your device

**Promotional text** (118/170): Record voice notes and read them back as text. Transcription runs entirely on your iPhone: your audio never leaves it.

**Keywords** (83/100): voice notes,recorder,dictation,transcribe,memo,audio,speech to text,private,offline

**Description**

MURMUR is a voice recorder that keeps everything on your device.

• Record voice notes with one tap
• Automatic transcription on device with Apple's speech models
• Rename notes, add tags and search titles, tags and transcripts
• Share or export audio and transcripts as .m4a, .txt or .md
• Available in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese

Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking, and your audio is never uploaded.

Transcription depends on the languages your iPhone supports for on-device recognition. If a language isn't available, MURMUR tells you; it never falls back to a server.
