Say it. Find it later. Keep it private.

MURMUR turns your voice notes into searchable text, on your iPhone. Your audio never leaves the device, and there is no account.

Join the beta (coming soon)

For spoken journals, lectures and interviews.

What it does

Private transcription

Text for every note, made on your iPhone with on-device recognition. If a language isn't available offline, MURMUR says so instead of sending audio to a server.

Search what you said

Find notes by title, tag or any word in the transcript, and jump to that moment in the audio.

Quick capture

Start recording before the thought is gone. In progress
